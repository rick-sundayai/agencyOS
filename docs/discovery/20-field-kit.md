# Field kit — estate agent session (#20)

One page. Print it or keep it open. Everything else is in the discovery guide.

---

## Opening (say roughly this)

> "I'm building software for agencies and I know recruiting well — I don't know your world at all,
> and I don't want to guess at it. I'm not selling you anything and I've got nothing to show you.
> I'd just like you to walk me through your last week in as much boring detail as you can stand."

Then ask permission to take notes, and start with **last Monday**.

---

## The spine

**Walk the week. Chronologically. Last Monday to last Friday.**

Interrupt only for:

- "What does that word mean?"
- "Then what happened?"
- "Who else was involved?"
- "Where did you record that?"

---

## Must-ask, even if time runs out

- Who is your client on a sale — seller, buyer, or both?
- Has anyone ever been selling with you *and* buying through you at the same time?
- What are the stages of a sale, in your words? Which ones can go backwards?
- What's a chain, and what does it do to your week?
- When exactly do you get paid, and what has to be true first?
- Can you do all the work and get nothing?
- Is your fee ever split with another agency?
- Where does the authoritative version of a listing live — your system, or somewhere else?
- What do you have to type into more than one place?
- Which follow-ups would you let something send without reading them first?
- What's the thing about this job that people outside it always get wrong?
- If I built software that did everything you've described, what would still be missing?

---

## Don't

- Don't pitch. Don't demo. Don't name the product.
- Don't ask "would you like a system that…"
- Don't ask about the general case. Ask about the last time.
- Don't fill silences.
- Don't correct them when their model differs from yours — that difference is the finding.

---

## Capture as you go

Four columns in your notebook. Nothing else.

| Their word | What it means | Recruiting equivalent? | Smells like divergence |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

Write **every** term they use that you wouldn't have used yourself. Those are the domain model.

---

## Five things to watch for

Tick any that appear — each one is a core-model change, not a detail:

- [ ] Same person is both client and counterparty
- [ ] A listing that came back / re-listed / went live more than once
- [ ] One sale's progress depending on another sale elsewhere (a chain)
- [ ] Commission split with another agency
- [ ] Compliance blocking the *transaction* rather than the *outreach*

---

## Before you leave

Ask: **"Can I come back to you with follow-up questions once I've written this up?"**

You will have them, and a second twenty minutes is far cheaper than a wrong core model.
